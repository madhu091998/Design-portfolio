Context

One contract. Hundreds of different versions.

Every salesperson signs a contract that sets out their targets and pay. The wording is the same for everyone. Only the numbers change.

An admin sends these to hundreds of people, every time someone joins, changes role or starts a new year.

Before

Digital contracts, manual work.

Signing was digital, through DocuSign. Getting contracts ready wasn't. Admins copied every person's numbers into a spreadsheet by hand, and couldn't see the finished contracts before sending them.

The insight

The numbers were already in Everstage.

Quotas, commissions and pay already lived in the product. Admins weren't creating information. They were looking it up and typing it in again.

How might we help admins fill contracts from the data they already have, without losing control over what gets sent?

Where AI fits

Figuring out what AI should actually do.

AI can look things up quickly. But a wrong number in a contract means someone gets the wrong pay. So AI does the looking up, and people make the decisions.

AI helps with

  • Finding values in existing data
  • Filling them in for everyone
  • Flagging what it couldn't find

Admins decide

  • What each blank should contain
  • Whether to use AI or type it in
  • What gets sent, after checking

Decision 1

Describe the blank, don't configure it.

Instead of matching spreadsheet columns, admins describe each blank in plain words, like “Q2 quota from New Business”. Typing / lets them point at the exact data. If AI can't help, they can still type, upload or set a default value.

Decision 2

Check a few before creating the template.

Before saving, admins pick a few people and see their finished contracts. Anything missing or wrong can be fixed right there, without starting over.

Decision 3

Review everyone in one table.

When it's time to send to hundreds of people, every value sits in one table. Missing ones stand out, and any cell can be edited or pasted in from a spreadsheet.

After

Describe it, check it, send it.

Everything now happens inside Everstage, including signing. No DocuSign account, no spreadsheet round-trip.

Reflection

What I took away.

Designing with AI isn't only about what AI can do. It's about helping people see what it did, and step in when something looks off. Sometimes the most important decision is what to leave to people.
